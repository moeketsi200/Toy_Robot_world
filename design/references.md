# Design references

## Battle Zone — controls feel

- [Decentraland Places](https://decentraland.org/places) — lists Battle Zone by AdventureLand at `battlezone.dcl.eth`.
- [Friendzone Buildathon winners](https://decentraland.beehiiv.com/p/friendzone-buildathon-winners) — describes Battle Zone as fast-paced aerial combat and says its controls work across desktop and the Decentraland Mobile App.
- [Battle Zone listing](https://mobile.dclexplorer.com/?realm=battlezone.dcl.eth) — describes short multiplayer rounds with guns, rockets, speed boosts, ammo crates, and drone patrols.

The owner selected Battle Zone as a reference for an **arcade-style robot combat feel**. The public descriptions support simple cross-device controls as the useful comparison; they do not publish Battle Zone's exact button mapping. This reference does not imply copying its aircraft movement or assets.
